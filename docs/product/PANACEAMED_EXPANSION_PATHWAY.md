# Panaceamed Expansion Pathway — one kernel, many domains

**Status:** owner-requested direction, 2026-11-09. Subordinate to the [Constitution](../../governance/doctrine/PANACEA_CONSTITUTION.md), the [99-axiom annex](../../governance/doctrine/PANACEA_99_AXIOM_CONSTITUTION.md), the [One OS doctrine](../../governance/doctrine/PANACEA_ONE_OS_LONGITUDINAL_CARE_DOCTRINE.md), the [current wedge](../../governance/doctrine/PANACEA_CURRENT_WEDGE.md) and every safety rule in `CLAUDE.md` §8. It sets direction and entry gates; it never relaxes a gate and never reorders the shipping priority.
**Companion:** [Horizon 2027 concept](PANACEAMED_HORIZON_2027_CONCEPT.md) (what is reachable before 2027).
**Nothing here is a clinical claim, a regulatory clearance, a business forecast or a promise of outcomes.**

## 1. The request, and the form it takes

The owner asked for a path by which Panaceamed can eventually serve every field of knowledge, enterprise, need, space and time, and every kind of wealth and guidance, as one unified whole rather than healthcare alone.

The unity is built as **one kernel, many domains**, not as one sovereign that manages everything. Three reasons, each already a rule of this repository:

1. **No claim to divine or total authority.** The 99-axiom annex forbids claiming divine attributes, total knowledge, infallibility or sovereignty over human life. A system that "manages all human activity" is a claim of that kind.
2. **No concentration of power.** One system governing every activity is a single point of failure and capture. Unity here means one *identity, consent, provenance and time model* that every domain reuses; it does not mean one authority that decides for people.
3. **Depth before breadth.** The Computational Human Platform and Invictus doctrines make validated vertical depth the default. Each new domain must earn entry; none is opened because it is imaginable.

```
THE RINGS (opened one at a time, each through the entry gates in section 5)
0  Body and health ........ NOW: longitudinal clinical encounter, Simple Screen, emergency card
1  Daily needs ............ nutrition, sleep, movement, environment, family and caregiving
2  Access and livelihood .. coverage and claims, work, learning, mobility, food and water security
3  Knowledge .............. evidence-graded science; teachings and guidance as attributed sources (section 6)

```

## 3. What "every field" means in practice

| Field | How it enters | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Fields of knowledge | As an evidence-graded library with sources and uncertainty, projected into the same state | Presented as settled when contested; invented citations |
| Enterprise | As verified providers and services on open contracts | Exclusive data deals; sale of a person's data |
| Space and time | As kernel dimensions (when, where, how precise), not as separate apps | Silent tracking; location without consent |
| Needs | As named, measurable needs of named users | Features without a user and a measure |
| Wealth and flows | Cost transparency, coverage and claim state, benefits the person chooses to share | Investment advice outside licensed adapters; denying care by profitability |
| Doctrine, teaching, guidance | As attributed sources the *person* selects (section 6) | Ranking beliefs, inferring a person's values, adjudicating religion |

## 4. The domain contract (what every domain must reuse)

A domain is accepted only with a manifest that a validator can check and that **fails closed** when anything is missing:

- **Named users and the real problem** they have.
- **Lawful sources** (licence or consent scope, jurisdiction, retrieval date, hash, use limits), reusing the dataset provenance gate (T-H27-03).
- **Kernel reuse:** identity, consent, provenance, time and units come from the kernel; a domain may not create its own patient or person truth.
- **Outcome measure** that can be falsified, and a **kill criterion** written before work starts.
- **Exit path:** everything the domain holds can be exported and its consents revoked, verified by test.

## 5. Entry gates and evidence levels

A ring or domain opens only when all five hold:

1. A named user and a real problem exist that no current capability handles.
2. The sources are lawful and consent is explicit.
3. The kernel is reused; no new silo.
4. A falsifiable outcome measure and kill criterion are written down.
5. An abuse and harm review exists, and the exit path is tested.

Evidence levels (never self-awarded): **E0** idea · **E1** repository evidence (tests, fixtures) · **E2** measured in a real setting with a named comparison · **E3** independently reproduced. A ring's expansion needs the previous ring at **E2** for its first slice. Reaching a level is recorded in the evidence ledger (T-H27-07); it is not asserted in prose.

## 6. Teachings, scripture, guidance and doctrine

These matter to many people's decisions, so they are supported with strict rules:

- **Sources, not authority.** Each item carries its source, tradition or school, edition or translation, licence and a *contested* flag where scholars or communities disagree.
- **The person chooses the lens.** Nothing is pushed; no ranking of traditions; nothing is inferred about a person's values or beliefs.
- **Translation, not ruling.** AI may explain or translate a passage; it does not issue rulings, certify correctness or replace the person's own advisers.
- **No mixing with clinical authority.** A teaching never overrides a safety rule, and a clinical statement never cites a teaching as evidence.
- **Safeguards.** Content for minors and for people in crisis follows the existing safety gates.

## 7. Pathway (an algorithm with gates, not a calendar)

`EXPLORE → GATE → ONE VERTICAL SLICE → PROVE (E2) → PUBLISH THE CONTRACT → OPEN TO THIRD PARTIES → NEXT RING`

- Ring 0 must show a verified outcome (T-H27-05) before Ring 1 starts building beyond what already exists. Existing daily-needs pages are *converged into the kernel first*, not multiplied.
- Within a ring, one slice at a time; breadth is discovery, depth is delivery.
- A domain that misses its kill criterion is narrowed or stopped, and the reason is kept.
- Later rings (2 to 5) are directions only. No task is opened for them until a named user, lawful source and measure exist; listing them as tasks now would be the feature sprawl this repository forbids.

## 8. Business and wealth

- Revenue follows completed, auditable outcomes and services, never lock-in. The person can always leave with everything.
- No sale of personal data, no exclusive data deals, no pricing or denial of care from health data.
- Economic claims (speed, cost, completeness) need a named comparison before they are made.
- Marketplaces list only verified providers and show how they were verified.

## 9. Hard guardrails

- Panaceamed makes no divine, all-knowing or sovereign claim, and does not manage people's lives for them.
- Consent, least privilege, revocation and access lineage wrap every domain; no covert monitoring.
- Measured, derived, simulated, reference and unknown stay distinct; empty is never zero.
- A failed safety gate blocks, defers or escalates; no domain, benchmark or revenue target compensates for it.
- Federation over concentration: standards and portability are preferred to ownership of other parties' systems.
