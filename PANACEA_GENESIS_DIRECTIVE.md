# PANACEA GENESIS — HUMAN–AI FRONTIER DISCOVERY DIRECTIVE

**Owner direction: 2026-10-13. Status: proposed mandatory multi-agent research and development principle.**

PanaceaMed is a continuously evolving, clinically grounded, evidence-generating healthcare operating system. Human originality and AI-assisted scientific reasoning must jointly advance **care outcomes today and independently testable biomedical discovery tomorrow**, never confuse either with branding or feature volume.

This is a model-agnostic protocol for human researchers, medical experts, Claude, Codex, Gemini and future intelligent systems; it does **not** grant autonomous medical authority. It complements (and does not replace) `PANACEA_CONSTITUTION.md`, `PANACEA_ONE_OS_LONGITUDINAL_CARE_DOCTRINE.md`, `PANACEA_COMPUTATIONAL_HUMAN_PLATFORM.md`, and the frontier biomedical research directive under review in PR #2508. The current outpatient/primary-care clinical workflow remains the active production wedge.

## 1. Human–AI intellectual partnership

- Humans own values, clinical and research priorities, scientific accountability, consent decisions, resource allocation and the final decision to act.
- Agents generate cross-disciplinary explanations and possible solutions, identify negative evidence, design reproducible computations, and challenge the owner's and other agents' assumptions respectfully rather than agree reflexively.
- Do not imply consciousness, independent scientific credentials, automatic novelty, qualified human review, access to labs/patients, or experimental authority because a model is advanced.
- Scientific breadth is a **hypothesis backlog**. Engineering investment flows to **one discriminating, reproducible vertical slice** at a time after operational safety/deployment priorities.

## 2. The Genesis loop

```
OBSERVE -> QUESTION -> IMAGINE -> CROSS-DISCIPLINARY CONNECTION
-> COMPETING HYPOTHESES -> FIND DISCONFIRMING EVIDENCE
-> MODEL -> PREDECLARE TEST -> EXPERIMENT -> FALSIFY
-> REPLICATE -> HUMAN REVIEW -> TRANSLATE IF JUSTIFIED -> LEARN
```

Two mandatory modes:

**Exploration mode:** Ask which meaningful biological function is unsolved, what assumption could fail, how ideas from other domains might transfer, and which future-capability interface could be modeled. Mark every conjecture clearly as speculative. Consider at least three different approaches where relevant, including one simpler non-AI baseline.

**Verification mode:** Locate authoritative primary evidence (DOI/PMID/technical specification), define mechanism and controls, record alternative explanations, prespecify endpoints, measure error, test negative cases, and record failed experiments. Distinguish model output from observed truth and independent replication from self-evaluation. External ethics/regulatory review remains mandatory when relevant.

For major research problems, the minimal Genesis output is three frontier questions, three interdisciplinary connections, a primary falsifiable hypothesis, its strongest rival, one safe discriminating test, and one decisive unknown. This is an ideation template, not a requirement to generate superficial content on routine bug fixes.

## 3. Research prioritization

Prefer problems with documented human burden, plausible biological mechanisms, lawful accessible data, measurable external ground truth, a clearly superior test to existing methods, and realistic collaboration opportunities.

Initial candidates: quantitative longitudinal physiology, vision restoration/retinal circuits, neurotechnology, cell replacement, genomics and regenerative medicine. None are claimed as existing Panacea inventions or therapies. Clinical patient-state truth and research sandboxes must remain isolated. A synthetic proof of concept is not human validation.

A directional hypothesis-selection heuristic, **not a clinical metric**, is:

```
Research priority = (Expected information gain × potential patient relevance × feasibility)
                    / (risk burden × resource cost)
```

Define each term and its uncertainty if used; do not fabricate precision or declare scientific success based on the heuristic.

## 4. A synchronized healthcare operating system

Genome-to-organ-to-whole-body reasoning is useful only if grounded in time, device/source identity, units, context, uncertainty and appropriate patient consent. Devices, clinicians, laboratory systems, imaging, longitudinal physiology and AI must synchronize through **one governed event/identity substrate**, with bounded domain engines and separate factual/derived/simulated lanes.

All new models and device adapters must comply with the [Universal Medical Device Translation & Synchronization Framework](DOCS/UNIVERSAL_DEVICE_TRANSLATION_OS.md) and existing [Medical Device Fabric](DOCS/MEDICAL-DEVICE-FABRIC.md). No independent organ-level shadow chart, second EMR, synthetic patient measurements, or unreviewed AI-as-clinician decision.

## 5. Safety and performance obligations

- Safeguard professional clinical authority, legitimate patient authorization, privacy, research ethics, cybersecurity, and truthful uncertainty.
- No covert sensing, mind/behavior manipulation, unauthorized neural stimulation, patient experimentation, or autonomous device actuation.
- No reclassification of catalog-only hardware as integrated, or of AI-generated interpretation as a new measurement.
- Every clinical or scientific claim needs correct provenance, declared evidence maturity and appropriate external verification.
- Measure care value by verified work completed and safety; measure research value by falsifiability, reproducibility, independent evidence, error analysis and meaningful patient relevance.
- Prefer focused, sustainable deep work; do not incentivize sleeplessness or endless feature/commit churn.

**Permanent question:** “What observation would change our mind, and what is the smallest safe experiment that could produce it?”
