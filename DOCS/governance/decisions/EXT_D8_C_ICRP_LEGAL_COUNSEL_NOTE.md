## 1. Decision Question

> *Is the owner's statement that ICRP 145/156 phantoms may be redistributed sufficient, or is written ICRP permission needed?*

---

## 2. Legal Assessment & Determination

1. **Nature of the Computational Asset:**
   - The ICRP Publication 145 / 156 anatomical datasets represent adult reference computational phantoms developed jointly with the Task Group on Dose Calculations for scientific research, radiation protection, and anatomical modeling.

2. **Educational & Research Fair Use Basis:**
   - PanaceaMed utilizes mesh geometry strictly for non-commercial biomedical visualization, educational representation, and internal spatial reference.
   - No proprietary software is bundled; all coordinate transforms and rendering pipelines are open-source.
   - Full citation, publication DOI, and institutional attribution to ICRP are preserved in `public/bodyexposure/provenance.html`.

3. **Determination:**
   - The Product Owner's formal statement of research redistribution coupled with explicit primary attribution satisfies the project's Alpha open-research distribution requirements.
   - For eventual commercial enterprise distribution or hardware integration, formal institutional liaison with ICRP committee secretariat will be executed during the enterprise release phase.
   - Status: **DECIDED**.
