# Unified Sport, Environment, Tactical Performance & Population Safety OS

## Canonical decision hierarchy

Panacea now treats sport, environment, tactical-athlete training, expedition/rescue and wearable telemetry as one operating system.

```
Population Safety
  -> source-valid synchronized telemetry
  -> sport/environment scientific analysis
  -> objective performance resilience
  -> actionable coaching
  -> sport-specific playbook
  -> reassessment
```

Performance never overrides safety.

## Shared kernels

- `universalSportOS.ts` — 50+ sport registry, scientific metrics and graph contracts.
- `wearableEnvironmentOS.ts` — terrain, weather, current, bathymetry, environment and authorized finding context.
- `sportAdventureRescueOS.ts` — diving/cave relay, rescue positioning, maritime/aviation companion context.
- `satelliteMeshNetworkResilience.ts` — defensive connectivity/failover.
- `tacticalAthleteOS.ts` — loaded mobility, agility, power, cognition, heat, sleep and evidence fusion.
- `performanceTelemetryEnvelope.ts` — canonical synchronized measured/estimated/derived/relayed telemetry.
- `performanceResilienceProfile.ts` — objective task-retention profile separated from psychometrics.
- `tacticalPerformanceCoach.ts` — actionable why/procedure/progression/reassessment coaching.
- `sportSpecificCoaching.ts` — sport-specific development playbooks.
- `populationSafetyOS.ts` — highest-priority safety gate.
- `performanceOperatingOrchestrator.ts` — the decision spine tying them together.

## Population safety prime directive

Priority order:
1. immediate life safety / emergency response;
2. participant, buddy, team and bystander protection;
3. route, venue, crowd and environmental hazard control;
4. communications, location and data integrity;
5. consent/privacy;
6. performance optimization.

A missing/unknown high-consequence safety state fails closed.

## Coaching rule

No analyzer should stop at a score.

Every actionable output should contain:
- what is limiting performance;
- why it matters;
- what to do next;
- progression;
- stop/modify criteria;
- reassessment timing;
- source/confidence boundary.

## Psychological resilience

Panacea does not infer a universal mental-toughness trait from wearables.

It keeps:
- validated psychometric/self-report instruments;
- objective task-performance retention under fatigue/pressure;

as separate evidence classes.

## Tactical scope

“Tactical” means elite human-performance rigor:
- strength/power;
- aerobic/anaerobic capacity;
- loaded mobility;
- agility;
- cognition/reaction;
- benign precision;
- sleep/recovery;
- environment/heat;
- team readiness.

It does not mean weapons optimization, human targeting, covert surveillance, pursuit/evasion operations or mission planning.
