Organ close-up models in this folder
====================================

Geometry: BodyParts3D 4.0
  (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS)
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Dataset: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
  Publication: Mitsuhashi et al. (2009), BodyParts3D: 3D structure database
  for anatomical concepts. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613

Packaging: ashemag/human-atlas (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 ashemag), which
converted the source OBJ meshes to metre/Y-up axes, simplified them with
meshoptimizer at a 0.2% relative error limit, quantised normals to signed
16-bit, and packed them into binary chunks with a manifest.

Adaptation in this repository: scripts/atlasOrgan.mjs selects the named parts
belonging to one organ, centres and scales them to a common size, and writes
one glTF-binary file per organ. Each mesh keeps its BodyParts3D name, so the
labelled points in the viewer are placed at the actual centroid of the mesh
they name rather than by hand.

This is an adult male reference anatomy. It is educational material, not a
clinical tool, and it does not represent every anatomical variation.
