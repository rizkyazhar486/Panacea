Specialty anatomy modules
=========================

Twelve modules, 497 individually named structures, cut from:

  BodyParts3D 4.0
  (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS)
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Publication: Mitsuhashi et al. (2009). https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613

Packaging: ashemag/human-atlas (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 ashemag).
Adaptation: scripts/atlasSystem.mjs (see src/lib/systemAtlas.gen.ts).

WHAT THIS REFERENCE ANATOMY DOES NOT CONTAIN, stated plainly because a
medical application must not imply coverage it lacks:

  - Female reproductive organs. BodyParts3D 4.0 as packaged here is an adult
    male reference. The obstetrics module therefore shows the bony pelvis,
    pelvic floor and pelvic vessels only.
  - The thyroid and parathyroid glands.
  - The middle ear (ossicles, tympanic membrane) in this module set; the
    ossicles do exist in the full-body figure's skeletal layer.
  - Lung parenchyma and pleura. The respiratory module has the airway tree
    down to segmental bronchi, the diaphragm and the chest wall, so segmental
    disease is shown at the bronchus that supplies the segment.

Educational material. Not a clinical tool, and not a substitute for imaging
of an individual patient.
