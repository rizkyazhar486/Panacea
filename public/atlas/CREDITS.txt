Specialty anatomy modules
=========================

Twelve modules, 497 individually named structures, cut from:

  BodyParts3D 4.0
  (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS)
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Publication: Mitsuhashi et al. (2009). https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613

Packaging: ashemag/human-atlas (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 ashemag).
Adaptation: scripts/atlasSystem.mjs (see src/lib/systemAtlas.gen.ts).

WHAT THIS REFERENCE ANATOMY DOES NOT CONTAIN, stated plainly because a
medical application must not imply coverage it lacks:

  - Female reproductive organs. BodyParts3D 4.0 as packaged here is an adult
    male reference. The obstetrics module therefore shows the bony pelvis,
    pelvic floor and pelvic vessels only.
  - The thyroid and parathyroid glands.
  - The middle ear (ossicles, tympanic membrane) in this module set; the
    ossicles do exist in the full-body figure's skeletal layer.
  - Lung parenchyma and pleura. The respiratory module has the airway tree
    down to segmental bronchi, the diaphragm and the chest wall, so segmental
    disease is shown at the bronchus that supplies the segment.

Educational material. Not a clinical tool, and not a substitute for imaging
of an individual patient.


ADDITIONAL MODULES FROM OTHER REFERENCE BODIES
==============================================

Four modules do not come from BodyParts3D as packaged by human-atlas, because
the structures they show are not in it. Each reference body has its own
coordinate space, so these modules stand alone and are never mixed into the
BodyParts3D modules — aligning them would mean guessing a transform, and a
guess in an anatomical atlas is a lie that looks tidy.

  paru     (Lungs & pleura)          Z-Anatomy, CC BY-SA 4.0
  tiroid   (Thyroid & parathyroid)   Z-Anatomy, CC BY-SA 4.0
  telinga  (Middle & inner ear)      Z-Anatomy, CC BY-SA 4.0

    Z-Anatomy — the libre 3D atlas of anatomy
    https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
    CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    Itself derived from BodyParts3D (DBCLS). These three files are
    ShareAlike: derivatives of them must carry the same licence.
    The same source already provides this application's full-body figure
    (see public/anatomy/CREDITS.txt).

  obgin    (Female pelvis)           HuBMAP HRA, CC BY 4.0

    HuBMAP Consortium / Human Reference Atlas,
    CCF 3D Reference Object Library, VH_Female v1.2
    https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-3d-reference-object-library
    CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    Uterus, ovaries, uterine tubes, vagina, their ligaments, urinary bladder
    and female bony pelvis, from the Visible Human female reference body.

Adaptation for all four: meshes were read from the published GLB files in
world coordinates, simplified by grid vertex clustering where they were too
dense for a phone (the cell size is recorded in scripts/atlasSystem.mjs),
centred and scaled as one piece so relative position is preserved, and written
as one file per module.

The library's placenta object was deliberately LEFT OUT: it is modelled apart
from the uterus rather than inside it, and a placenta floating twenty
centimetres above the womb would teach a false anatomy.
