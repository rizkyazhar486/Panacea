3D anatomy assets in this folder (skeletal.glb, muscular.glb, cardiovascular.glb,
nervous.glb, visceral.glb) are derived from:

  Z-Anatomy — The libre 3D atlas of anatomy
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
  License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

  Which is itself derived from:
  BodyParts3D — The Database Center for Life Science
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
  License: CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan

Authors (Z-Anatomy): Kousaku Okubo (original BodyParts3D model), Gauthier Kervyn
(design, 3D, anatomy), Marcin Zielinski (Blender add-on), Lluis Vinent (app dev).

What was changed for Panaceamed.id: exported five system-separated GLB files
(skeletal, muscular, cardiovascular, nervous, visceral) from the original
Z-Anatomy Blender project, applied Meshopt mesh compression, and replaced the
original Blender node-based shaders (which depend on Blender-viewport-only
effects) with flat PBR materials colored per anatomical structure type
(muscle red, artery red, vein blue, nerve yellow, bone tan, etc.) so the
models render correctly in a web WebGL viewer (three.js).
