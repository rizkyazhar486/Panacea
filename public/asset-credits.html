Asset sources and licensing

Sources and attribution for media published by PanaceaMed. This page documents provenance; it is not a blanket licence or an assertion that all third-party redistribution rights have been verified.

Body Exposure anatomy and reference data

Full source-by-source credits, derivative notes and mesh licences are available on the Body Exposure provenance page, and locked third-party renderer/decoder MIT software notices are published on the Body Exposure 3D renderer software notices page. Source groups include Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D (CC BY-SA 4.0 for Z-Anatomy-derived meshes), HuBMAP (CC BY 4.0 where declared), University of Denver Visible Human geometry (CC BY 4.0), and other individually credited reference datasets. CC BY-SA 4.0 legal terms require attribution and appropriate sharing of adaptations.

ICRP Publication 145 and 156: source credits and derivative descriptions are recorded on the Body Exposure page, but the owner's internal approval is not proof of redistribution rights from ICRP. The ICRP official reuse policy says permission may be needed even for free-to-read materials. Do not interpret the source notice as a licence grant.

Fetal MRI atlas: Uus et al. (2024) identifies the KCL fetal body atlas repository. The dataset-specific licence requires verification against the upstream dataset record; a journal article licence does not automatically extend to every data file.

Video, brand images and typography

Video source inventory documents brand-intro.mp4 and history.mp4 as project-rendered Remotion works. The recorded design uses Plus Jakarta Sans; the typeface's upstream SIL Open Font License 1.1 is independently published by Tokotype. The exact binary version used for the rendered video is not proven without the original composition.

SVG image source inventory identifies five project-created vector images and their git authorship. The repository is not automatically public-domain or licensed for third-party reuse.

Unresolved provenance: logo-mark.png and media/tutorial-video.mp4 have git upload history but their full creation and underlying-material rights have not yet been documented. Do not infer ownership or permission from the upload alone.
