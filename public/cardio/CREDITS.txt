Cardiovascular model (cardio.glb)
=================================

131 individually named vessels and heart structures, cut from:

  BodyParts3D 4.0
  (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS)
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Dataset: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
  Publication: Mitsuhashi et al. (2009), BodyParts3D: 3D structure database
  for anatomical concepts. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613

Packaging: ashemag/human-atlas (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 ashemag), which
converted the source meshes to metre/Y-up axes, simplified them with
meshoptimizer at a 0.2% relative error limit, and packed them into binary
chunks with a manifest.

Adaptation in this repository: scripts/atlasCardio.mjs selects the vessels
that carry the teaching, centres the whole set as one piece so that the
relative positions stay true, colours each structure by its OXYGEN CONTENT
rather than by "artery red, vein blue" (so the pulmonary artery is blue and
the pulmonary veins are red), and computes a centreline for every vessel
along its longest axis so blood can be animated flowing through it.

Mesh names are the BodyParts3D names, unchanged. src/lib/cardioAtlas.gen.ts
carries the same names, which is how a condition in src/lib/cardioPathology.ts
can light up the exact vessel it damages.

Adult male reference anatomy. Educational material, not a clinical tool.
