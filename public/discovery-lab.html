Research readiness = 0.24·mechanistic coherence + 0.24·replication + 0.20·clinical relevance + 0.18·safety characterization + 0.14·provenance. This is not regulatory approval likelihood.

Trust layer

Every clinical or research claim should answer: what was measured, what was simulated, what evidence supports it, what remains unknown, and who must review it.

M
Measured

Observed patient or experimental data

Labs, imaging, validated questionnaires, sensors and trial measurements retain source identity, time and acquisition context.

D
Derived

Transparent calculations

Derived features expose formulas, units, missingness and uncertainty rather than hiding transformation behind a generic “AI confidence”.

S
Simulated

Hypothesis-generating model state

Simulation outputs are visually distinct and cannot silently become patient truth, diagnosis or treatment orders.

E
Evidence

Publication provenance

Claims backlink to source literature and preserve study type, population, limitations and relevance to the current question.

H
Human review

Qualified reviewer gate

High-risk clinical translation remains draft-only until the appropriate clinician/research governance process reviews it.

Δ
Uncertainty

Unknowns are first-class data

Out-of-distribution state, sparse evidence, conflicting literature and missing data widen the uncertainty envelope instead of being treated as normal.
