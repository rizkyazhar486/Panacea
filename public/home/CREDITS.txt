atlas-body-front.webp, atlas-body-holo.webp, atlas-body-turn.webp

Source: Z-Anatomy atlas, the same meshes shipped in public/anatomy (skeletal.glb and muscular.glb).
Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 (see public/anatomy/CREDITS.txt for the full attribution of the source atlas).
How it was made: rendered by scripts/bangun/bake-home-body.mjs, one anterior view, transparent background,
no geometry edited. The hotspot positions in src/domains/body-exposure/data/homeBodyAnchors.ts are projected
from the named atlas meshes with the same camera.
Truth class: reference anatomy. It is not the viewer's body and not a patient image.
atlas-body-holo.webp is the same render pass with a Fresnel rim material per layer (bone, muscle, cardiovascular,
nervous); geometry unchanged.
atlas-body-turn.webp is the holographic pass rendered from 24 directions (every 15 degrees about the body's own
vertical axis) and packed left to right into one sprite; geometry unchanged. Home fetches it only when the body is
turned, and shows hotspots only on the front view they were projected for.
